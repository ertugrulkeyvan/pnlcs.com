# PRODUCT.md — pnlcs.com

register: brand

## Product purpose
pnlcs.com is the public website of PNLCS, a free, MIT-licensed, self-hosted hosting billing platform and client portal (an open-source WHMCS alternative) maintained by the Panelica team and volunteers. The site's job: let a hosting operator understand in one visit what PNLCS does, see the real product, judge whether it is mature enough, and install it or try the demo. Second job: turn operators into contributors.

## Users
- **Primary:** owners and technical staff of small and mid-size hosting companies (shared hosting, resellers, VPS sellers). Many run WHMCS today and pay for it. They read on a desktop at work, compare options carefully, and distrust marketing. They want: supported panels and gateways, what is tested, how to install, how to migrate.
- **Secondary:** developers, translators and designers who might contribute.

## Brand
- Owned by Panelica. Colours come from the official Panelica theme: navy `#405189`, teal dot `#0ab39c`, hero navies `#1a2140` / `#2d3766` / `#1e2850`. Logo: "PNLCS" wordmark + teal dot.
- Voice: plain, specific, technical, honest about limits. Like a well-run open-source project (Laravel, Forgejo, Plausible docs), never like a SaaS landing page. No slogans that say nothing; no hype words.
- English site.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS landing pages: gradient heroes, big-number stat rows, identical icon cards, "X, not Y" slogans, CTA on every section.
- WHMCS-marketplace clutter.
- Claims that cannot be verified in the PNLCS source or docs.

## Strategic principles
1. Show the product (real screenshots), not illustrations.
2. Every number and feature must be verifiable in the repository or docs.
3. Say what is untested. Credibility comes from candour, not adjectives.
4. The demo and the install guide are the two actions that matter; everything else links to docs.
