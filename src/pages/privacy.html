Privacy policy

This page covers the pnlcs.com website. PNLCS installations run by hosting companies have their own privacy policies.

Last updated 2026-10-06

Who is responsible

pnlcs.com is run by Panelica, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company.

Contact for anything on this page: info@panelica.com

When you visit the site

The site is served through Cloudflare, which receives your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and block attacks. Your browser also loads a few things from other services:

  • Fonts from Google Fonts
  • Project figures and contributor pictures from GitHub and shields.io
  • Google Tag Manager, which sets no cookies itself and only loads the tools below after you accept

These services see your IP address when your browser contacts them. They set no cookies for pnlcs.com.

Cookies and measurement

With your consent, the site uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to see how pages are used, and the Meta Pixel to measure ads. They set cookies and send usage data to Google, Microsoft and Meta. Nothing of this runs until you choose Accept.

Your choice is kept in your browser’s local storage, so the question is not repeated on every page. You can change it at any time.

The discovery call form

When you send the discovery call form, your name, email, company, country and message are sent through Web3Forms to the PNLCS team by email. They are used only to reply to you and to prepare a quote, and they are deleted when they are no longer needed for that, or earlier if you ask.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or deletion, and you can object to its use or withdraw your consent at any time. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Contact for these requests: info@panelica.com
