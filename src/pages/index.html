What happens to a renewal

PNLCS runs billing on a schedule, so nobody has to remember to send an invoice or switch off an unpaid account. These are the defaults; every number is a setting.

Billing lifecycle in the docs

  1. 14 days before

    Renewal invoice

    Created and emailed to the customer.

    invoice_days_before_due = 14

  2. 3 days before

    Stored card charged

    If you turn auto-charge on. Up to 3 attempts, 3 days apart.

    AutoChargeDaysBefore = 3

  3. Due date

    Reminders

    Unpaid invoices turn overdue and reminder emails go out. An optional late fee applies after 3 days.

    LateFeeMinDays = 3

  4. 3 days after

    Suspended

    The service is suspended on the server. Paying unsuspends it within 30 minutes.

    AutoSuspensionDays = 3

  5. 30 days after

    Terminated

    Only if you enable it. Off by default.

    AutoTerminationDays = 30

Every morning, in this order

The order matters. A customer whose stored card pays at 06:45 is never suspended at 07:00, and is not reminded to pay at 08:00.

06:00Create the day’s renewal invoicespnlcs:generate-invoices
06:30Mark unpaid invoices overduepnlcs:mark-overdue
06:45Charge stored cardspnlcs:auto-charge
07:00Suspend services past the grace periodpnlcs:auto-suspend
07:30Apply late feespnlcs:apply-late-fees
08:00Send payment reminderspnlcs:payment-reminders
