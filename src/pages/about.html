About PNLCS

PNLCS is a free, self-hosted billing platform and client portal for web hosting companies, resellers and infrastructure providers. It is built in the open and released under the MIT License.

Why it exists

The Panelica team wanted an open, self-hosted billing system that integrates natively with Panelica and works well with other control panels too. Nothing like that existed, so they built one, in their spare time, alongside their main product.

It deliberately follows WHMCS concepts, so a WHMCS team can find its way around. Unlike WHMCS, it is MIT licensed.

We built PNLCS because we needed it ourselves, and we believe the software a hosting business runs on should not be a monthly rent. It should be a tool you own, understand and can improve. That only works if it is built together. The Panelica team, maintainers of PNLCS
